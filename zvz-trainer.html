Main lesson

Muta dominates the early mirror.

The first 6 rounds are usually Muta-focused. Your first job is to avoid losing the Muta phase so hard that every later transition is late.

Sometimes skipping round 2 is correct if it helps you hit the right Muta timing. Early Infestor and early Queen are not reliable main answers.

Transition checklist

  • If enemy Viper wins value, switch Hydra and add your own Viper.
  • If enemy goes Ultra, transition to Ultra also.
  • Sell Mutas if needed to afford T3 before round start.
  • Hydra by minute 7 is still usually correct.
  • Lategame is often dominated by Ultra or Lurker.
